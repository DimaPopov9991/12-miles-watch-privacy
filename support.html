12 Miles Watch – Support

12 Miles Watch tells you when your vessel enters or leaves the 12-nautical-mile zone off the coast.

Contact

For questions, problems with the app or subscription help, write to popov9991@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of what happened.

Common questions

Notifications do not arrive in the background. Open iOS Settings → 12 Miles Watch → Location and choose "Always", make sure Notifications are allowed, and keep monitoring turned on in the app.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription? Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose 12 Miles Premium. You can also restore a purchase from the subscription screen in the app.

Is the 12-mile zone an official border? No. The zone is calculated from GPS and offline coastline data and is provided for guidance only.
