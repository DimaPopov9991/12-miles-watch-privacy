12 Miles Watch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Summary

12 Miles Watch does not collect, sell or share your personal data. The app has no account, no advertising and no analytics.

Location

Your location is used on your device only, to measure the distance to the coastline, to tell whether you are inside the 12-nautical-mile zone and to estimate your course and speed. It is not sent to us or to any third party. The coastline data used for these calculations is stored in the app.

Background location

When monitoring is on, the app keeps reading your location in the background so it can notify you when you cross the zone boundary. You can turn monitoring off at any time with the Stop monitoring button, or change the location permission in the iOS Settings.

Notifications

Notifications about entering or leaving the zone are created on your device. No notification server is used.

Subscription

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We do not receive your payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether your subscription is active.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your settings and the monitoring state on your device. Deleting the app removes this data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page. For questions, use the developer contact details shown on the App Store page of 12 Miles Watch.
