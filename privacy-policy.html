12 Miles Watch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy covers the 12 Miles Watch app for iOS and for Android.

Summary

12 Miles Watch has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and we do not sell or share your personal data. Your location stays on your device. The only data kept on a server is the free-trial record on Android, described under “Data stored”.

Location

Your location is used on your device only, to measure the distance to the coastline, to tell whether you are inside the 12-nautical-mile zone and to estimate your course and speed. It is not sent to us or to any third party. The coastline data used for these calculations is stored in the app.

When the app starts it asks for location and notification permission. Without them it cannot warn you.

Background location

When monitoring is on, the app keeps reading your location in the background (on Android with an ongoing notification) so it can notify you when you cross the zone boundary. You can turn monitoring off at any time with the Stop monitoring button, or change the location permission in the system settings.

Notifications

Notifications about entering or leaving the zone are created on your device. No notification server is used.

Map and coastline data

Android: the map background is loaded over the internet from OpenStreetMap servers (© OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the Open Database License). Those servers see your IP address and the map area you view; your GPS position is not sent to them.

iOS: the map background is Apple Maps and is loaded over the internet. Apple receives the map requests under Apple’s privacy policy. Your GPS position is not sent to us.

Coastline data and the 12-nautical-mile zone lines (OpenStreetMap, Natural Earth, Marine Regions/VLIZ) are stored in the app, so distance, zone detection and alerts work without internet.

Subscription and free trial

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (iOS) or Google Play (Android) through your store account. We do not receive your payment details. The app only learns from the store whether your subscription is active. The app is free to use for 7 days before a subscription is needed.

Data stored

On your device: your settings, the monitoring state and the date your free trial started. On iOS the trial date is kept in the Keychain, so it survives reinstalling; nothing is sent to a server.

On Android, so that the free trial does not restart when the app is reinstalled, the trial start date is also stored on a Google Firebase server together with a device identifier (Android ID). The identifier is not linked to your name or account. Deleting the app removes the local data; the server record stays until we remove it on request (write to the address below).

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from them.
